# S18 — Material that can be skimmed

**Design status:** Closed (reader approved 2026-11-01)
**Implementation:** Not implemented, deployed, tested, or verified in this repository.

## Original scenario
> You encounter material that may not warrant close reading. You want help judging what deserves attention, without having the agent decide for you.

## Approved decisions (designed jointly with S15)

### S18-D01 (joint S15-D07) — Option C: Goal-aware recommendations
**Status:** Approved. **Decision:** Agent may recommend close reading, skimming, or skipping based on the current reading goals and plan, mathematical background, conceptual importance, relevance to subsequent material, and technical burden. Recommendations have a concise mathematical rationale; reader retains authority. **Alternatives:** no recommendations; generic technicality-based recommendations. **Rationale/evidence:** Reader chose C when discussing S15-D07/S18.

### S18-D02 (joint S15-D08) — Option A: Section-entry timing
**Status:** Approved. **Decision:** Recommendations are presented at the start of a section rather than repeatedly at each proof or passage. **Alternatives:** local recommendations on encounter; both section and local recommendations. **Rationale/evidence:** Reader chose A.

### S18-D03 (joint S15-D09) — Option C: Conceptual summary
**Status:** Approved. **Decision:** A recommended skim/skip explanation includes why the material is lower priority, its role in the surrounding mathematics, and a concise conceptual account of the principal idea, potentially revealing the big-picture proof strategy without technical derivation. Further detail is available on request. **Alternatives:** justification alone; justification plus mathematical role only. **Rationale/evidence:** Reader selected C.

### S18-D04 (joint S15-D11) — Option C: Adaptive section overview
**Status:** Approved. **Decision:** Initially provide a short section-level overview and reading recommendation; the reader can request more detailed breakdown conversationally. Do not impose a detailed reading schedule. **Alternatives:** brief fixed overview; annotated section outline by default. **Rationale/evidence:** Reader chose C and pointed out overlap with S14; avoid duplicating established adaptive conversational principles.

### S18-D05 (joint S15-D10) — Option A: No in-reading interruption
**Status:** Approved. **Decision:** No unsolicited checks or re-recommendations while the reader is reading; the agent only responds if approached. **Alternatives:** periodic check-ins; selective interrupts. **Rationale/evidence:** Reader chose A.

## Shared interaction principle
The agent is a high-level, natural-language coordinator, not a micromanager. It can proactively propose sensible reading strategy at section entry; options such as reading, skimming, skipping, reconstructing, or checking understanding may be suggested in text rather than enforced via mandatory menus. The interactive S15 proof roadmap remains an acceptable visual tool. Preserve independent mathematical discovery and user control.

## Approved reader experience and closure
At section entry the agent offers a concise, goal-aware reading strategy and identifies material that merits close attention versus a conceptual skim or skip, with brief reasons and mathematical role. Reader can ask for more detail, disregard suggestions, or change course. Agent remains silent during reading. User reviewed the combined S15/S18 experience and said “should be good” (2026-11-01).

## Boundaries / deferrals
Long-proof roadmap and return to skipped proofs belong to S15; difficult-definition help to S14. Avoid claiming automated knowledge of exact reader activity, timing, or comprehension. No app behavior implemented or browser-verified here.
