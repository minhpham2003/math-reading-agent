# S12 — Reading a normal conceptual unit

**Design status:** Closed (reader approved 2026-11-01)  
**Implementation:** Not implemented in this repository; no deployed or browser-tested S12 flow verified.

## Scenario and boundary

> The session is underway. The agent wants you to read a specific passage of the PDF, understand its purpose, and then continue the discussion.

S12 specifies the ordinary reading loop, not specialist proof/definition handling, reading-plan editing internals, or session-memory mechanics.

## Approved reading-loop decisions

### S12-D01 — Adaptive guidance with reader control
**Status:** Approved (2026-11-01).  
**Decision:** Adapt orientation to passage difficulty and existing agreements, but respect prior reader preferences by default. When extra preparation seems useful, **offer it before giving it**, rather than unsolicited hints.  
**Alternatives discussed:** Rigid minimal guidance; unsolicited extra orientation; optional extra orientation.  
**Rationale / approval evidence:** Reader selected adaptive behavior, then explicitly chose Option 2 — Offer guidance.

### S12-D02 — Reader-driven interaction
**Status:** Approved (2026-11-01).  
**Decision: Option A — Reader-driven interaction.** Once a reading passage is assigned, remain silent without automatic check-ins or inferred completion; remain available for reader questions.  
**Alternative:** Periodic check-ins.  
**Rationale / approval evidence:** Reader chose A.

### S12-D03 — Conversational completion
**Status:** Approved (2026-11-01).  
**Decision: Option B — Conversational completion.** No required Done Reading button. The reader may ask questions during reading, and a question does not imply completion. Natural utterances can indicate completion or resumption.  
**Alternatives:** Explicit completion button; both.  
**Rationale / approval evidence:** Reader chose B, explaining questions may arise along the way.

### S12-D04 — Reader-controlled return
**Status:** Approved (2026-11-01).  
**Decision: Option B — Reader leads resumption.** Preserve the passage and discussion context but do not automatically steer back to reading after a question or exploration. Resume when reader indicates.  
**Alternative:** Agent prompts return after resolving questions.  
**Rationale / approval evidence:** Reader chose B.

### S12-D05 — Context-aware post-reading discussion
**Status:** Approved (2026-11-01).  
**Decision: Option C — Context-aware response.** Upon a natural indication of finishing, use the agreed plan, conversation, and material to decide whether to discuss, check understanding, or offer to continue; do not quiz mechanically.  
**Alternatives:** Always start a discussion; always ask reader to choose.  
**Rationale / approval evidence:** Reader chose C.

## Related conversational agreements recorded during S12 (cross-cutting, not additional requirements specific to the ordinary reading loop)

- **S12-D06 — Selective intervention (Option C):** Generally let reader finish thoughts; maintain rigor; intervene selectively when significance warrants it. Reader explicitly referenced Mathematical Discussion and Problem-Solving Preferences.
- **S12-D07 — Concise listening mode:** When the reader is developing reasoning, respond minimally (e.g. “Go on”).
- **S12-D08 — Offer before contributing:** At a likely stopping point, offer to contribute rather than immediately taking over; an explicit request for checking can be answered directly.
- **S12-D09 — Persistent listening mode:** Once in listening mode, remain there until reader signals otherwise; brief factual questions do not automatically end the mode.
- **S12-D10 — Automatic or explicit activation (Option C):** Infer listening mode naturally or activate via “Hear me out” / similar. Existing mathematical discussion guidelines remain the authoritative behavioral foundation.

These are approved conversational preferences, but their general architecture belongs to cross-cutting agent interaction design, not to further expansion of S12.

## Deferred decisions

- **S12-D11 — Plan revisions (Option C):** Reader prefers agent-proposed changes requiring explicit approval; plan-editing workflow to be handled in the relevant plan-management scenario.
- **S12-D12 — Selective summary (Option C):** Preserve important discoveries, meaningful attempts and mistakes, unresolved questions, and reading location, but not every exchange. Session-summary/memory design belongs in its dedicated scenario.
- **S12-D13 — Summary update timing:** **Not decided**. The reader flagged that the conversation had drifted out of S12 before choosing.

## Approved end-to-end experience

The agent directs attention to a source-anchored passage with agreed guidance. Reader reads undisturbed, may initiate discussion or questions at any point, and can explore without automatic return prompts. Agent follows reader-centered mathematical discussion guidelines. Reader signals completion conversationally; agent responds according to the goals and context without a fixed quiz.

**Invariants:** Dashboard-first; dark mode; left agent/right PDF; nearly full-screen workspace; collapsible reading plan; New Session popup (all inherited from S02).

## Closure (2026-11-01)

**Goal achieved in design:** An ordinary conceptual-unit reading interaction has been agreed and explicitly closed by the reader.  
**Implementation / deployment / tests:** Design only; no S12 application code, deployment, browser test, or verified behavior is claimed.  
**Deferred:** Conversational-mode architecture (D06–D10), plan management (D11), session memory and update timing (D12–D13); specialist reading cases remain separate scenarios.
