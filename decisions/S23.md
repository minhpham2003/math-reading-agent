# S23 — Missing prerequisite

**Status:** Design in progress — approved decisions documented, scenario not closed  
**Updated:** 2026-11-01

**Original scenario:** The reader no longer remembers a prerequisite needed for the current passage; review may take several minutes.

## Approved design decisions

### S23-D01 — Direct reminders; proactive tangent suggestion for substantial reviews
**Status:** Approved  
**Classification:** Both (primarily agent behavior)  
**Decision:** Simple forgotten definitions or brief clarifications receive a direct answer inline. When an ongoing clarification becomes substantial, the agent proactively suggests a tangent. An explicit request for a major review may prompt the suggestion immediately. A mere question does not automatically become a tangent.

**Agent obligations:** Distinguish quick recall from substantial review using conversational context; answer simple recall requests directly; recognize extended review; offer a brief, noncoercive suggestion without redirecting the mathematical inquiry; never create a tangent solely on its own initiative. An explicit reader instruction to create a tangent is authorization to execute, not merely to suggest (see S24-D09–D10).

**UI obligations:** Keep simple reminders in the same conversation. Avoid contextual lists of canned intellectual prompts. Permanent tangent controls may coexist with conversational instructions under S24.

**Alternatives:** Always discuss inline; mark a detour visually without branching; suggest a tangent for substantial discussion. The reader approved proactive suggestion after substantial discussion.

**Rationale / approval evidence:** Reader: “if I just forget a definition, the agent should simply remind me of it. But if the discussion becomes substantial, or I ask for a big review of something, then that's where the branching comes in”; subsequently selected **A** (suggest proactively when substantial).

**Implementation:** Not established. **Verification:** Not tested.
