# S07 — Selecting a Reasonable Reading Range

Status: In design
Last updated: 2026-11-01
Scenario: Reader selects a reasonable section/page range; the agent identifies several conceptual units.

## Approved design decisions

### S07-D01 — Analyze after entering the workspace
**State:** Approved (2026-11-01)
**Decision:** Choose option B: keep the New Session popup simple. Clicking **Create Session** opens the existing nearly full-screen reading workspace, where the agent assesses the selected range and presents its conceptual structure. Preserve the agent-left / PDF-right layout.
**Alternatives considered:** A — expand the creation popup with analysis; C — show a compact analysis in the popup before entering workspace.
**Rationale:** Keep session creation separate from source-grounded reading analysis; avoid popup complexity; let the agent and reader inspect the PDF together.
**Approval evidence:** Reader replied “B” to the explicit A/B/C design question.
**Implementation:** Not started in this documentation repository.
**Verification:** Not performed.

## Open design decisions

### S07-D02 — Present a proposed reading plan
**State:** Approved (2026-11-01).
**Decision:** Option C. On entering the workspace, the agent analyzes the selected range and populates the existing collapsible reading plan as a **proposal**. The reader may inspect it, request modifications, or accept it with one click. Guided reading of the first conceptual unit starts only after acceptance.
**Rationale:** Combines an immediately useful, source-grounded conceptual structure with reader control. Avoids a separate plan-generation step and keeps approval lightweight.
**Approval evidence:** Reader replied “C” to the explicit A/B/C design question.
**Implementation:** Not started.
**Verification:** Not performed.

### Alternatives considered
**Options previously offered:**
- A — conversational breakdown first; reading plan generated after confirmation.
- B — automatically populate existing collapsible reading plan and give a short explanation.
- C — generate a structured *proposed* plan that the reader can accept or modify before beginning the first conceptual unit.

Option C has now been explicitly approved. Details of the plan contents and editing interaction remain to be designed.

## Constraints inherited from S02
Dashboard-first navigation; New Session popup; dark mode; agent on left / PDF on right; nearly full-screen workspace; collapsible reading plan.

## Scope boundary
S07 concerns a reasonable selection and its conceptual structure. Excessive scope belongs to S08; cutting across an important mathematical unit belongs to S09.
