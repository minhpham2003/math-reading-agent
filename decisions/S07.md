# S07 — Selecting a Reasonable Reading Range

**Status:** In design  
**Last updated:** 2026-11-01

**Scenario:** The reader selects a reasonable section or page range. The agent evaluates its scope and identifies several conceptual units.

## Approved design decisions

### S07-D01 — Analyze after entering the workspace

**Status:** Approved — 2026-11-01

**Decision: Option B — Workspace-first analysis**

Keep the **New Session** popup simple. After clicking **Create Session**, the reader enters the nearly full-screen reading workspace.

Within the workspace, the agent:
- Analyzes the selected reading range.
- Assesses whether the scope is reasonable.
- Identifies its conceptual structure.

The existing layout is preserved: **agent on the left, PDF on the right**.

**Alternatives considered**
- **A — Popup analysis:** Expand the New Session popup to show the analysis before creating the session.
- **C — Two-stage preview:** Show a compact analysis in the popup and the full analysis in the workspace.

**Rationale**

Keep session creation separate from analysis of the mathematical source. This avoids making the popup overly complicated and allows the reader and agent to inspect the PDF together.

**Approval evidence:** The reader chose B in the A/B/C discussion.

**Implementation:** Not started.  
**Verification:** Not performed.

### S07-D02 — Present a proposed reading plan

**Status:** Approved — 2026-11-01

**Decision: Option C — Proposed reading plan**

After entering the workspace, the agent analyzes the selected range and generates a **proposed reading plan** in the existing collapsible panel.

The reader can:
- Review the conceptual units.
- Request modifications.
- Accept the plan with one click.

Guided reading of the first conceptual unit begins **only after acceptance**.

**Alternatives considered**
- **A — Conversational:** Present the analysis in chat and generate a plan after confirmation.
- **B — Automatic:** Populate the reading plan and allow reading to start without explicit approval.

**Rationale**

The proposed plan gives the reader useful structure immediately while preserving control over the learning strategy. One-click acceptance avoids unnecessary friction.

**Approval evidence:** The reader chose C in the A/B/C discussion.

**Implementation:** Not started.  
**Verification:** Not performed.
