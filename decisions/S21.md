# S21 — Quick clarification that should stay inline

**Design status:** Closed, approved 2026-11-01.
**Implementation status:** Design documented only. Not implemented, deployed, tested, or verified.

## Original scenario
You ask a small question that can be resolved in one or two exchanges without leaving the main reading path.

## Goal
Handle brief mathematical clarification in the existing conversation without interrupting reading.

## Approved decisions

### S21-D01 — Option A — Inline discussion
**Status:** Approved.
**Decision:** The reader asks a quick mathematical question in the existing agent chat. The agent responds according to the reader's Mathematical Discussion and Problem-Solving Preferences document: follow the reader's reasoning, carefully check arguments, and avoid unsolicited hints or solutions. No separate branch, popup, or mode change is triggered. The PDF and reading plan stay in place.
**Alternative:** Open a dedicated branch or separate clarification interface.
**Evidence:** Reader said that for a quick exchange the agent should simply help according to the preference document.

### S21-D02 — Option B — Remain silent after exchange
**Status:** Approved.
**Decision:** After answering, the agent waits for the reader's next input; it does not automatically resume guidance or suggest next steps.
**Alternative:** Automatically resume guided reading.
**Evidence:** Reader explicitly chose “silent.”

## Closure
The reader asks and resolves a brief question inline, with the main reading context preserved. The agent then remains silent until the reader initiates continuation. S21 is designed and closed.
