# S14 — A dense definition

**Design status:** In progress (2026-11-01)  
**Implementation:** Not implemented or verified in this repository.

## Scenario and boundary

> You reach a definition whose formal statement is short but conceptually dense. You need motivation, examples, and enough space to think about it yourself.

This scenario addresses support for a difficult definition. Ordinary reader-driven reading and discussion follow S12.

## Approved decisions

### S14-D01 — Passive awareness of the current PDF page
**Status:** Approved (2026-11-01).  
**Decision: Current-page awareness without interruption.** The PDF viewer should make the currently viewed page available as context to the agent while the reader reads silently. This is contextual information, **not** a license to interrupt, assume which definition the reader is examining, or infer completion. The agent also knows the assigned passage from the reading plan.  
**Alternatives discussed:** Knowing only the assigned reading; tracking the viewed PDF page; knowing the exact paragraph or definition automatically.  
**Rationale / approval evidence:** The reader clarified that the agent is not continuously interacting while they read and then explicitly said, “yes knowing the page is useful.”

## Pending questions

- When the reader says “this definition,” how should the agent identify the exact definition? Current-page context, explicit PDF selection, clarification, or a combination remains undecided.
- No decision has been made to track eye gaze or an exact word/paragraph position.

## Relationship to S12

S12-D02 requires silence and no automatic check-ins during assigned reading. S14-D01 preserves that agreement.
